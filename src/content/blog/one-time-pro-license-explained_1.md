<p class="answer">Soft Clipper has no free tier and no "upgrade to Pro" button — there is one product, sold once, for $39. That single payment unlocks every feature (AI moment detection, speaker-tracking reframing, captions, unlimited exports, no watermark) for one PC, with no renewal and no separate Pro plan to buy into later.</p>

## Why "one-time Pro upgrade" is the wrong mental model

Most desktop and web tools that use the phrase "upgrade to Pro" work as freemium software: a limited free version, and a payment that unlocks the rest. Soft Clipper isn't built that way. There's no free version with a watermark, a clip limit, or a locked feature set waiting behind a paywall. You pay $39 once, before you use the app, and that payment already includes everything — AI moment detection across the whole video, the virality score and hook titles, smart 9:16 reframing, burned-in captions in six styles, unlimited clips and exports, and free updates to the version you bought.

If you've come from a tool that sells itself in "Free" and "Pro" tiers and you're looking for the equivalent upgrade path here, the honest answer is that it doesn't exist, because the free tier it would upgrade from doesn't exist either.

## What one licence actually covers

A licence activates the app on one PC. That's the whole unit — not a seat count, not a usage quota. If you replace or reinstall your machine, you release the licence from the old one inside the app and activate it on the new one. Working across two machines regularly (a desktop and a laptop, say) needs two licences; there's no separate multi-seat product to negotiate for a small number of editors. For more than five, [Soft Clipper](https://softclipper.pro) will set up a single invoice rather than five separate card payments.

Once activated, the app works offline. AI moment detection and transcription use your own Google Gemini API key by default, which has a free tier that covers ordinary use — anything beyond it is billed by Google directly, never by Soft Clipper. You can also switch transcription and analysis to run entirely on your own PC, with no account, no key and no bill at all, at the cost of a slower first run.

## What "upgrade" means when it does apply

The one place the word "upgrade" genuinely applies is version numbers, not tiers. Point releases and bug fixes to the version you bought are free, indefinitely — that's part of what the one-time payment buys. If a genuinely new major version ships later, existing customers are offered an upgrade price rather than being asked to pay full price again, and the version you already own keeps working either way, since nothing about it depends on an ongoing subscription or a server checking in to keep features unlocked.

That's a meaningfully different guarantee to a subscription tool, where losing access to the paid tier can mean losing access to clips still sitting in the service's library. With Soft Clipper the processing happens locally and the output files are yours from the moment they're exported, licence or no licence going forward.

## When a one-time licence isn't the better fit

To be direct about the limits: a one-time licence assumes you'll keep using the tool. If you only need it for a single video, one launch or one short campaign, a monthly subscription elsewhere may genuinely cost less overall — [the full cost comparison, month by month](https://softclipper.pro/compare/one-time-vs-subscription/) lays out exactly where the break-even point sits. And because Soft Clipper is a Windows and Mac desktop app, it needs a PC that can encode video; it isn't the right fit if you're working from a phone, a Chromebook, or a browser only.

## If you're still unsure

Soft Clipper offers a 14-day refund, no reason required — email from the address you bought with and it's refunded in full. Because the app runs locally rather than through a login you could otherwise just cancel, that refund window functions as the trial period a "free to Pro" tool would normally give you.
